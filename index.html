Frequently asked questions

Who is Claude for Busy Professionals written for?

Non-technical professionals who use a computer for most of their work, such as managers, consultants, freelancers, analysts, marketers, administrators and small business owners. It assumes no coding and starts from the basics.

What is the BRIEF method for prompting Claude?

BRIEF is a five-part checklist for writing prompts: Background, Reader, Instructions, Examples and Format. A quick question needs one or two parts; anything you will send to a client or manager deserves all five.

Which Claude model should a professional use?

Start with Sonnet for most work. Switch to Opus when a task is complex or a mistake would be costly, and use Haiku when speed matters more than depth. Claude Fable 5.1 is Anthropic's most advanced model for long, demanding projects; it uses your allowance much faster, so most professionals rarely need it.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Every workflow is written in plain language and runs in the Claude apps. Where code helps, for example calculating numbers in a spreadsheet exactly, you simply ask Claude to use code and it writes and runs it for you.

How do I get Claude to write emails that sound like me?

Paste five samples of your own writing of the same type and ask Claude to write a short style guide describing your sentence length, tone, openings, closings and habits. Save it in your preferences or a project, and include it whenever you ask for a draft.

How can I use Claude at work safely?

Never paste passwords, card or bank numbers, or confidential data you are not allowed to share. Use a work account for work data, give Claude source documents instead of relying on its memory, check every number you will repeat, and keep sending, sharing, buying and deleting behind your own approval to guard against prompt injection.

Is the book up to date?

The 2027 Edition covers the Claude 5.5 models (Opus, Sonnet and Haiku) and Claude Fable 5.1, with features, plans and prices checked in October 2026. It focuses on methods that keep working when menus change, and an update log records changes between editions.
